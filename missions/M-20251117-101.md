## Summary
Create a CLI script that scaffolds worklogs from `templates/WORKLOG_TEMPLATE.md`, pre-filling metadata and removing placeholders to enforce consistent structure.

## Motivation
Manual worklog creation leads to inconsistent formatting and missing fields, making automation difficult. A generator ensures every mission starts with the correct structure and enables hooks to verify metadata.
